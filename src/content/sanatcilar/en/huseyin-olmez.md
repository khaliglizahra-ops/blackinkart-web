Hello, I'm Hüseyin Ölmez. My journey began with piercing in 2014 and continued in 2017 with tattooing, which I taught myself. In 2020 I opened the Black Ink Art Tattoo Piercing studio on Aşkabat Caddesi (formerly 7th Street) in Çankaya, Ankara, and turned that passion into a full-time craft.

What keeps me committed to this work is that the learning never ends, and that every client brings a new style and a new story with them. Signing a piece that someone will carry on their body for the rest of their life is both a serious responsibility and a rare privilege.

At Black Ink Art, every piece is made without compromising on hygiene standards, by genuinely listening to the client's story, and with a great deal of care. I would be glad to design the tattoo or piercing you have in mind with you.

— Hüseyin Ölmez, Founder of Black Ink Art
