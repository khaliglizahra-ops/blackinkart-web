A cheek, or dimple, piercing creates the effect of a dimple in the face and is placed from the inside of the mouth outwards. Because the technique pierces both the oral mucosa and the outer cheek tissue, it takes longer to heal than other surface piercings.

Sticking to an antiseptic mouthwash routine in the first days reduces the risk of infection. For the general rules, see our [piercing aftercare](/en/piercing/piercing-aftercare) page.
