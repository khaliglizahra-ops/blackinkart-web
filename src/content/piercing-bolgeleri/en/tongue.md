Among oral piercings, the **tongue piercing** is one of the fastest to heal and becomes comfortable sooner than the others. **Smiley** and **Marley (tongue web)** are placed through the upper or lower lip frenulum, while **venom** and **frowney** require symmetrical placements at different points on the tongue.

Swelling during the first week is normal. Sucking on ice a few times a day and sticking to cold, liquid food brings the swelling down quickly. For full aftercare, see our [piercing aftercare](/en/piercing/piercing-aftercare) page.
