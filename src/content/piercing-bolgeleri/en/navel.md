The navel piercing has one of the longest healing times, because the area is in constant contact with clothing. We recommend avoiding high-waisted clothing, or anything that will rub, for at least **2 months** after the piercing.

Because this area moves so much, bioplast jewellery can make healing easier for a first piercing — see [piercing materials](/en/piercing/piercing-materials). For the aftercare routine, see our [piercing aftercare](/en/piercing/piercing-aftercare) page.
