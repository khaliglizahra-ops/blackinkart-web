Because genital piercings are a matter of privacy, we do not show portfolio photographs on this page. For information about the procedure and pricing, please contact us directly.

The tissue in this area has a high blood supply, so light bleeding for 2–3 days after the piercing is normal. We recommend using a single-use pad during this period and keeping the area clean. As with every piercing we do, all work is carried out with sterile equipment and single-use materials — see [hygiene and safety](/en/hygiene-and-safety).
