The ear is the most popular piercing area and offers by far the widest variety. The **lobe** heals fastest and is the least painful option. Cartilage placements — **tragus, helix, conch, rook, snug, daith** — take longer to heal and are somewhat more sensitive. Two-point placements such as **industrial** and **transverse lobe** need a little more care than single piercings.

We recommend titanium for a first piercing — see [piercing materials](/en/piercing/piercing-materials). Because cartilage heals more slowly than other tissue, it is especially important to stick to the aftercare protocol. For the details, see [piercing aftercare](/en/piercing/piercing-aftercare).
