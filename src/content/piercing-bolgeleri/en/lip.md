The lip area offers a wide range, from single piercings such as **labret, Medusa, Monroe and Madonna** to multiple combinations like **snake bites, angel bites, shark bites** and the legendary **T-Rex bites**. Because of the risk of contact with the gums and tooth enamel, **bioplast** jewellery is often recommended here — see [piercing materials](/en/piercing/piercing-materials).

For placements that contact the inside of the mouth, using an antiseptic mouthwash during the first week and avoiding yeasted foods will speed up healing.
