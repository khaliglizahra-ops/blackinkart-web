An eyebrow piercing is a popular choice for anyone wanting to add a distinctive detail to their face. Because the skin here is relatively flexible compared with other surface piercings, healing usually goes smoothly — though you should still be careful of knocks and friction in the first few weeks.

Before we begin, we work out together the point that best suits the shape of your eyebrow. Our general [piercing aftercare](/en/piercing/piercing-aftercare) protocol applies.
