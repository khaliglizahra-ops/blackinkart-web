Different cultures around the world developed different ways of tattooing, and several are still practised today:

- **Western technique (machine)** — The modern method, first patented in England in the late 1800s, using a pedal-controlled needle mechanism. This is the technique we use at Black Ink Art.
- **Japanese technique (Tebori)** — A traditional hand method using bamboo rods tipped with needles.
- **Samoan technique** — A Pacific method in which ink is placed by tapping a bone-toothed comb into the skin.
- **Hand poke** — Freehand work with a needle and ink, without electricity.

## Non-permanent options

For anyone who wants to try before committing to a permanent tattoo, temporary options exist — henna, sticker tattoos, airbrush tattoos. These fall outside the services our studio offers.

## Why the machine technique?

Because the needle moves at high frequency and under control, machine tattooing hurts less and gives the most consistent result in terms of line clarity and colour saturation. That is what has made it the most widely used method in the world today.
