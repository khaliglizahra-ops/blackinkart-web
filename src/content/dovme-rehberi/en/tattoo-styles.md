Deciding which style to get can be harder than you expect. These are the styles most often chosen at our studio:

- **Realism** — Photographic levels of detail. Choosing the right artist matters enormously here.
- **Blackwork** — Geometric or abstract designs in black ink only, with dense fills.
- **Minimalist** — Small, restrained, fine-line designs, usually carrying a personal meaning.
- **Traditional (Old School)** — Bold outlines, vivid colour and iconic motifs: roses, compasses, swallows.
- **Neo-Traditional** — The clean linework of traditional tattooing combined with a richer colour palette.
- **Dotwork** — The image is built entirely from dots. A technique that demands patience.
- **Geometric** — Crisp lines, symmetrical patterns, mostly plain black ink.
- **Watercolour** — Flowing colour transitions that bring the feel of watercolour on paper to skin.
- **Tribal / Maori** — Dense black patterns drawn from tribal motifs thousands of years old.
- **Japanese (Irezumi)** — Traditional Japanese imagery: dragons, koi, cherry blossom.
- **Lettering** — A name, a date or a line that means something. Font and placement decide the result.
- **Portrait** — A realistic likeness of a person's face. This demands a high level of skill.

You can see real examples of these styles in our [portfolio](/en/portfolio). If you cannot decide which style suits you, we can work it out together in the consultation before your appointment.
