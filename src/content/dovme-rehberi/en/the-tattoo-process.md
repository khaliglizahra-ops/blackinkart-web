A good tattoo is a process that moves through the right steps in the right order. Here is how we work at Black Ink Art:

1. **Choosing the design and consultation** — We settle the motif, the size and the placement together, taking into account your skin and how the tattoo may spread over time. For visible areas such as hands, face and neck we talk this through in more detail first.
2. **Preparing the design** — Once approved, the design is prepared for transfer onto paper.
3. **Preparing the skin** — The area is cleaned, shaved with a single-use razor if needed, and disinfected with an antiseptic solution. The design is then transferred onto the skin.
4. **Sterile setup** — The work area is covered with single-use drapes, and single-use needles and inks are laid out. Every item is opened from its packaging in front of you.
5. **The tattoo** — Outlines first, then filling and shading. The skin is kept taut so the lines come out clean, and is wiped down regularly throughout.
6. **First dressing** — The fresh tattoo is covered with a dressing that keeps the skin moist while still letting it breathe.

What happens afterwards matters just as much — see our guide to [tattoo aftercare and healing](/en/tattoo/tattoo-aftercare).
