How your tattoo looks depends as much on how you care for it as on how it was done. Human skin renews itself in about 21 days, but this varies from person to person, so we recommend keeping up your aftercare for a full **30 days**. You can use either of the two methods below — both are valid.

## Method 1: Surgical dressing

The dressing applied over a fresh tattoo keeps the skin moist while still letting it breathe. Leave it on, without removing it, for **5–6 days**. After that, take the dressing off and keep moisturising the tattoo regularly so it does not dry out.

## Method 2: Cling film

Once the tattoo has been cleaned thoroughly, apply a repair cream (such as Bepanthol Plus) or petroleum jelly and wrap it in cling film. For the first 2 days the film should be changed every **2 hours**. At each change, clean the skin, let it air, then re-apply cream and re-wrap. After 2 days the film is no longer needed.

## General rules (for both methods)

- Avoid direct contact with tap water for the first week. In the shower, coat the area generously with petroleum jelly and use lukewarm or cool water where you can.
- Never scratch the tattoo, and never pick at any scabbing.
- Avoid tight clothing that will rub the area, and keep your clothes clean.
- The skin must stay moist throughout healing — do not let it dry out.

If you notice redness, excessive swelling or any discharge, contact us straight away.
